FAQ

Does HousingNav require an account?

No. Core tools work locally on device without an external account.

Where are saved listings and notes stored?

Favorites, listing notes, checklist progress, and saved results are stored locally on the user's device.

Are calculator results final approvals?

No. HousingNav provides planning estimates and organization tools. Final eligibility, rent approval, and lease-up decisions are made by the administering agency.

How do I report a problem?

Email support with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
